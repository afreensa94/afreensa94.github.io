Who I Am

I'm a UX researcher with an M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction from DePaul University, focused on mixed-methods research: usability testing, moderated interviews, heuristic evaluation, card sorting, and tree testing.

My research process starts with the right method for the actual question, not a default toolkit — evaluative methods when I need to know if something works, generative methods when I need to know how something should be structured.

I've also built research tooling from scratch, which means I understand research methodology structurally, not just as a set of steps to execute.

That same rigor is where my interest is most focused: interactive and narrative games. I've designed and iterated on narrative game systems with real community partners — including a game built with the Chicago Refugee Coalition — refined through repeated playtesting and revision cycles alongside the research work shown here.
