Who I Am

I'm a UX/UI designer with an M.S. in Human-Computer Interaction from DePaul University, working across interactive narrative, game UI, and applied product design.

My design process is grounded in research, but the deliverable is always the interface: wireframes, prototypes, and shipped visual systems, not just findings.

I care about interaction design that holds complexity without overwhelming the person using it — pacing, structure, and visual hierarchy doing real work, not just decoration.
